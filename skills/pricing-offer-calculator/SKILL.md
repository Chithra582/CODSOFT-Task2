# Pricing Offer Calculator Skill

## Purpose
Calculate transparent order subtotals and evaluate promotional discounts.

## Capabilities
- Sum quantities across flavors (Chocolate $10, Strawberry $8, Vanilla $6).
- Apply volume discounts (e.g. 15% off orders of 3 or more scoops).
- Provide instant itemized pricing breakdowns.

## Execution Guidelines
1. Ingest flavor selections and quantities.
2. Calculate base price subtotal.
3. Apply qualified discount rules.
4. Output final estimated total.
